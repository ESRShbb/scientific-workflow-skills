# Panel-specific scientific checks

## Western blots and gels

Verify lane order, conditions/doses, protein/isoform identities, loading controls and quantification denominator. Align lane headers and numbers to the same lane centers; align quantitative row labels to the corresponding baseline. Place isoform labels beside the correct bands. Brackets include only their actual lanes.

Do not redraw bands, selectively enhance signal, erase lanes or distort images for appearance. Cropping/splicing requires truthful provenance and disclosure consistent with destination standards. Distinguish hiding an unused white margin with a clipping mask from altering the scientific crop. A tidy panel cannot establish biological independence or justify a normalization denominator.

## Quantitative plots, curves and heatmaps

Keep axis scales/units, transformations, summary/replicate meaning, error definitions, significance rules, effect direction and statistical population tied to the analysis. Do not add a cutoff or remove observations to make a trend cleaner. Related panels may share fonts while retaining different scientifically justified ranges and mappings.

Bars, error bars, points, significance brackets and category labels must refer to the same groups. Bubble area versus diameter mapping must match the stated method. Provide clearance for edge bubbles/error bars and leaders for crowded labels. Do not read an endpoint from an illustrative redrawn curve.

For heatmaps, preserve matrix/transform, row and column order, clustering leaf order, group annotations, color anchors and display range. A diverging palette's neutral point must have the correct meaning. Do not remove dendrograms, reorder labels or select pathways as mere styling when the change alters analysis.

## Microscopy and other scientific images

Preserve channel identity, fields, treatment labels, aspect ratio, crop and calibration. Uniform image scaling requires matching scale-bar geometry. Arbitrary resizing does not change the reported biological magnification or create resolution. Distinguish representative fields from quantitative sample size and analyses. Display changes must preserve evidence and be disclosed where relevant.

## Chemical and mechanistic diagrams

Verify atom/bond/charge/stereochemical information and attachment points against the structure source. Preserve reaction connectivity and evidence-backed conditions. Use layout to show direction and convergence clearly; decorative proximity is not evidence of binding, causality or a reaction step.

Protein/docking/MD views need model origin, residue numbering, pose/interaction identity and measurement units. Label computational models, experimental structures and inferred interactions distinctly. Rotations can aid visibility but must not conceal a contradiction or imply a different pose. A diagram of a proposed mechanism must not masquerade as an experimental structure.
